## What remains for the original chordal problem

This closes the arbitrary split-neighborhood subproblem posed in your message. It does not yet close Erdős #81 for all chordal graphs.

For a general chordal graph, partitioning the split shadow around P leaves the internal edges of G−P uncovered. Adding their partition cost separately is exactly the failed interface identified earlier. The proof above does not establish the stronger inequality needed to pay for those edges.

The false FC and split-profile inequalities are not needed for the split result, and should not be reinstated for that final assembly.

## What remains

The remaining obligation is consequently an existence theorem for the coupled packet selection. It cannot be replaced by adding cp(J) to the split bound, and it does not require reviving the false FC benchmark.
